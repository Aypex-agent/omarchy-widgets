# Corsair

A pirate survivors-like for omalauncher. Your ship's guns fire on their own: drag anywhere to steer,
sink what comes for you, gather doubloons and level the ship up. Every upgrade shows on the ship:
more masts, cannons along the hull, parrots circling it, a dragon on the bow.

- **Goal:** each of four dangerous biomes (Coral Shallows, Frozen Reach, Haunted Fog, Burning Sea) has
  a boss guarding a relic. Linger and it wakes; beat it in its own waters to claim the relic. With all
  four, sail to Treasure Isle and slay the Sea King, then sail on endlessly if you like.
- **Upgrades:** 38 in common, rare and epic, offered three at a time on each level-up, and 4
  legendary ones sold only at rare island outposts.
- **Foes:** 13 kinds across five biomes, elites, swarm events, and five bosses. Every boss beaten
  makes the sea harder.
- **Harbor:** gold you bring home (all on a win, half if you sink) buys permanent upgrades.

Back or the pause button opens the menu (resume, restart, abandon). Needs the launcher's Linux:
the game's clock is a small shell loop (`date` and `sleep`, from coreutils).
