# Omagara v1.9.1

Niagara-inspired OWL 21 home face for [Omalauncher](https://github.com/SimonSchubert/omalauncher). No additional packages required.

## Features

- **Alphabet:** Elastic A–Z navigation, frequently launched app previews, hide apps from the worm without hiding them from Search, usage-based ordering by default or alphabetical ordering, plus persistent per-app moves within letters, a Settings cog, optional opposite-edge input, and configurable haptics.
- **Apps:** Buffered full-height browser with elastic ends, adjustable scale, padded A–Z headings, and two compact bottom settings actions under a Settings heading. Holding the cog previews only Omagara settings and Omalauncher settings. Empty Search places the most recently opened app first.
- **Notifications:** Grouped cards, optional per-app hiding (Omagara only) and alert compression, optional timestamps, swipe feedback, expandable actions/replies, and native scrolling with the rest of the home page.
- **Appearance:** Clock, date and event alignment with the notification column (or screen center), automatic centering when the home alphabet is hidden, adjustable text/shade colors, opacity, and corners.
- **Settings:** Home, Clock, Apps, Alphabet, Notifications, Appearance, Holidays, and Backup; 12/24-hour time, icons, fonts, and Reduce Motion.
- **Media:** Optional native playback and connected-device preview, compact by default. Home editing offers a separate **Expand/Compact** toggle and smaller **↕ Art size** handle with Reset. Dragging adjusts compact album artwork from 32 to 52dp without changing modes; expanded mode retains its larger artwork, song details, and Bluetooth output information. Pinned apps and notifications reflow below. The existing music-app shortcuts chevron remains independent of preview size and play/pause.
- **Utilities:** Calculator in Search, calendar, next alarm, timer, stopwatch, and world clocks.

Slide onto the cog below the last alphabet letter (with haptic feedback) to navigate to the bottom settings menu. Select Omagara settings or Omalauncher settings there. On **Home**, hold Search to enter edit mode; holding Search while browsing the alphabet still opens Omagara settings.

Time format, **Abbreviate weekday and month**, and **Show battery percentage beside date** are independent controls under **Clock** in Omagara settings. Both toggles default OFF (`Monday, November 16`). Abbreviation only shows `Mon, Nov 16`; battery only shows `Monday, Nov 16 100%`; both ON show `Mon, Nov 16 100%`. Battery uses the native Android source. OWL does not expose Android's system time format. **Home** offers two buttons to set the persistent default: Omagara (`home.home-page = 0`) or the original tile home (`home.home-page = 1`). An optional background command changes `~/.config/omalauncher/style/style.toml` without opening a terminal. Omagara displays success or failure in Home settings and saves a backup. Omalauncher Linux is required for these two buttons, not for the face itself. Separate page-navigation buttons do not change the default. World clocks appear under Clock, corner rounding under Appearance, and export/import/reset under Backup.

## v1.9.1 changes

- **Inline shade visibility:** Home editing now offers individual eye/slashed-eye controls beside the clock, alphabet Worm, media player, notification headings and cards, pinned app labels (when present), and Search/buttons. Shades toggle immediately; Home ✓ saves changes, while × restores their previous values. The existing Appearance settings retain fine-grained opacity controls.
- **All Shades shortcut:** The Home editing button appears opposite the alphabet worm, hides every shade at once, and can restore previous per-element choices. Its ON/OFF/MIXED indicator tracks the shades for currently visible elements, including when their eye controls are toggled individually.
- **Cleaner Home editor:** Relocate the Worm position handle and its shade eye beneath pinned apps, above pin-row alignment controls. Remove it from the clock area. Place the notification-card eye beside Alerts gap instead of over notification content.
- **Clock and date:** Expand the clock shade to the full Home content width, keep the clock inside the shade at its smallest size, allow the compact date/battery row to align right, and increase spacing before the battery percentage.
- **Media artwork:** Tapping the album art opens the current media app, like tapping the song details.
- **Compatibility:** Keep previous preferences, older settings imports, and existing Home editing behavior. No new packages or permissions.

## v1.9 changes

- **Date & battery:** Independent optional weekday/month abbreviation and native battery percentage in the date header; both default OFF. Old settings backups without either toggle still import.
- **Home edit controls:** Hold Search on Home, use Settings → Home → Edit Home layout, or hold a pinned app/folder and choose **Move app…** / **Move folder…**. Search becomes **✓ Save** with a Settings cog above; **× Cancel** appears to the left of ✓ when the alphabet is on the right, and to the right when it is on the left, while ✓ stays at Search's original location. In Home edit mode, drag any pinned app or folder immediately with one gesture, without first tapping it; dragging another pin commits the previous preview. Tapping a pin still selects its row for alignment. ✓ saves the session; × restores its original layout and spacing. No inline ✓/× beside Home pins. Alphabet app-move ✓/× now appear in the Search position, mirrored with the alphabet side; they no longer travel beside the floating app.
- **Layout editor:** Drag the body of **↕ Clock gap**, **↕ Alerts gap**, **↕ Art size** (while media or connected shortcuts are visible), or **↕ Worm** directly for continuous adjustments. Slim handles emphasize the active drag. Clock and Alerts have temporary edit-only clearance lanes so they do not cover the date or notification heading. During Home editing, the Worm adjustment handle sits beside the clock rather than over media or pinned apps, on the clock's right for a right-side alphabet and on its left for a mirrored alphabet. Compact clock mode reserves space for it. With the clock/date/event header disabled, the Worm handle remains accessible at the screen edge. Every handle retains its own **Reset**, affecting only that position. The Home destination outline is lighter and inset. The temporary clearance vanishes on ✓ Save or × Cancel. The Worm handle appears in Home editing and in explicit Alphabet layout editing, including while moving an app there. It stays hidden for ordinary long-press app moves outside the editor, and leaving Alphabet browsing cancels an unsaved app move. Alphabet retains its separate Done and app-move controls.
- **Header:** **Clock settings → Compact clock & date** switches between full and one-line clock/date layouts. A compact/expand control also appears temporarily while editing the Home layout or moving a home pin, never during normal use. The clock font scales to available height, width, and tightened Home spacing (minimum 18dp; compact minimum 14dp).
- **A–Z placement:** Move an app horizontally to keep its icon on the right; left remains the default. Side choices persist per app, independent of Search, home pins, and vertical ordering. While an alphabet app move is active, the worm no longer accepts navigation gestures. The normal hold-menu **Move app…** action and **Edit Alphabet layout** use the same full-list move mode. In Alphabet layout editing, dragging any app immediately selects and moves it, without an initial tap. Starting a drag on another app commits the previous preview automatically; the selected app remains within its own letter. Drag an app near the top or bottom edge and keep holding it there to scroll continuously without moving your finger, driven by OWL's frame callback. Scrolling stops when the app moves away from the edge, the drag ends, or the selected letter's boundary is reached; it never crosses into another letter. If the resulting order matches the position without that app's manual overrides, saving removes its manual-position marker (the small line beside the app). Holding or dragging a second app commits the current move and switches to it. Alphabet move ✓/× replaces Search until the current preview is saved or cancelled.
- **Home pin-row alignment:** Pinned rows default left-aligned. During Home editing, tap a pinned app or folder to select its row: **Row →** shifts that row right and **← Row** shifts it left. **All →** shifts all pins right (including mixed layouts), changing to **← All** once all pins are right. The buttons sit below the pinned rows and appear only during editing. Their arrows reflect the side actually displayed, including when rows with different prior alignments were joined. Alignment changes are available only inside Home edit mode; the Home pin hold menu still offers **Move app…** / **Move folder…** to enter editing, while **Apps → Pinned apps & folders** retains pin management and row grouping. An individual horizontal stack moves together without reordering its members; standalone pins move independently. Alignment persists across restarts and existing v1 settings backups. Automatic wraps follow the stack's alignment. Moving a pin into another stack inherits that stack's alignment; splitting it into its own row keeps its preference. The Home × restores row alignments from the start of that edit session. **Left-aligned standalone rows retain icon → text**; right-aligned standalone rows display text → icon so the icon is closest to a right-side alphabet. Stacked icon order is unchanged, and the floating move preview uses the matching label orientation.

- Default to **Most used**, with Alphabetical still selectable. A one-time upgrade migration switches existing saved sort choices to Most used; later manual selections are preserved.
- Long-press an A–Z app and choose **Move app…**. The original row lifts with a spring and subtle floating motion. Drag vertically or tap a destination; surrounding apps spring into their projected positions and an outline marks the snap slot. Inline **✓** saves; **×** discards the preview. Only that letter's order changes; Search remains automatic.
- A slim accent line marks moved apps; long-press one to **Reset automatic position**.
- Replay per-app moves without freezing the other apps' automatic sorting. Hidden, work-profile, and cloned apps retain distinct keys; Search is unaffected.
- Export, import, and reset manual moves and media preview size in settings; older v1 backups remain accepted and default the media preview to compact.
- Create named folders by holding an app or using **Apps → Manage folders**. Add/remove apps, rename/delete folders, and move folders or their app entries up/down.
- Named folder rows follow the last A–Z app section, before Settings, with a folder silhouette and a centered 2×2 preview of installed app icons. Tap a folder to open a floating, scrollable popup with normal app actions; hold the row to edit it.
- A single folder shortcut above the worm's Settings cog jumps to the folder section. The worm keeps its original overall height regardless of folder count, and the cog stays at the bottom.
- Fix worm hit testing between the last letter, folder shortcut and settings cog so the letter no longer reactivates at the boundary.
- Pin apps and folders without a fixed total limit. Horizontal rows automatically wrap to fit the available screen width, while standalone pins show labels and stacks hide them. **Move app…** previews a magnetized grid in the actual home layout: the lifted icon floats while neighbors shift aside, an outlined slot shows the drop location, and release snaps there. The floating preview uses the destination's icon and label layout; joining a stack hides the label; the global Home ✓/× buttons control saving and canceling. Move controls, target highlights, and drop outlines follow Appearance → App labels corner rounding (square when OFF). Horizontal drag targets follow the hovered icons, not fixed-width steps; only the selected pin changes stacks. Moving downward can make that pin a standalone row without detaching neighbors. ✓ saves the projected order and row breaks; holding another pinned app or folder saves the current arrangement and immediately selects the held pin for movement. Native shortcuts and folder assignment remain available. Pin or unpin a folder from the folder manager or editor; tapping its home pin opens the same folder popup as the alphabetical browser. Renaming keeps the pin, and deleting the folder removes it. Pinned apps show a theme-accent notification dot when alerts are present; tap it to expand notifications or hold it for the app menu.
- Home is one vertically lazy-scrolling document: clock, date, calendar event, media, flexible pinned rows and native notification cards move together, while the alphabet worm and Search remain fixed. Clearing an alert reflows the rest upward. The old notification-start setting is retired from the UI but remains accepted in older settings backups.
- Folders persist in settings backups without removing their apps from the normal app list or Search.

## v1.8 changes

- Hide apps from the alphabetical worm while keeping them accessible in normal Search. Sort apps within letters by recent launcher usage.
- Adjust notification start height automatically or manually. Hide selected apps' notifications only in Omagara, or compress repetitive alerts. Hidden notifications remain in Android's shade and are unaffected by Omagara Clear all.
- Keep a compact media player with two-line song titles, fixed play/pause control and simple chevrons by default. Home settings can independently expand the media/Bluetooth card, and a temporary smaller Home editing handle adjusts artwork size without changing the selected compact/expanded mode. Expand music-app shortcuts manually using their existing chevron when external audio is connected.
- Align the clock, date and event with the media/notification column by default, or choose screen center. Hiding the home alphabet centers the content automatically.
- Keep left/right alphabet input independently configurable. Identify duplicate app names by icon, package ID and profile in settings.
- Preserve older settings backups and the existing notification gestures, holidays and Reduce Motion support.

## v1.7 changes

- **Shading:** Independent opacity for the clock/header, alphabet, notification headings/cards, app labels, and Search/X buttons. Theme, grey, black, white, or custom hex shade color.
- **Text:** Custom hex text colors and readable high-contrast defaults.
- **Corners:** Restored compact header and notification-card rounding, refined alphabet-worm caps, and added an all-on/all-off rounding control.
- **Gestures:** Passive L-shaped six-dot swipe hint (optional), better separation from notification scrolling, and scrollable empty footer area.
- **Controls:** Independent Left/Right placement for alphabet and Search, plus X position (follow Search, Left, or Right). X aligns with Search when placed on the opposite side.
- **Settings:** Save/export the new appearance and navigation preferences, while retaining compatibility with older backups.
- **Default-home command:** Retains the upstream v1.6.1 inline command for transparent store review.
- **Holiday Easter eggs:** Enabled by default on Halloween (Oct 31), Christmas (Dec 25), and New Year's Day (Jan 1), using the phone's local date. Tap the small decoration near Search for a one-time animation (rolling pumpkin, snowfall, or fading gold sparkle). Opening the alphabet re-arms it. Preview each holiday or turn off the feature under Holidays. Reduce Motion skips the animations.

## v1.6 changes

- **Clock:** Added manual 12-hour and 24-hour display (24-hour default) for the home clock, alarms, calendar, and world clocks. Removed the unsupported System option; older System preferences import as 24-hour.
- **Notification timestamps:** Added a show/hide toggle and formatting that follows Omagara's clock choice.
- **Notification alignment:** Reserved the expand arrow's 44dp slot so timestamps stay put for both expandable and ordinary notifications.
- **Notification pull:** Added matching 64dp rubber-band resistance and 240ms rebound at the header and scroll footer, respecting Reduce Motion. Native scrolling and swipe actions remain intact.
- **Settings layout:** Replaced the long settings page with Home, Clock, Apps, Alphabet, Notifications, Appearance, and Backup categories. Added clear ON/OFF states, selected options, and live clock-format examples.
- **Settings navigation:** Removed nested expanding menus. World clocks live under Clock, corner rounding under Appearance, and import/export/reset under Backup.
- **Default home:** Distinguished temporary page navigation from the persistent Omagara (page 0) or original tile home (page 1) setting.
- **Default-home actions:** Replaced clipboard-only and full-screen terminal controls with optional background updates to `style.toml`, backup creation, and in-page success/error feedback. Omalauncher Linux is required only for those actions.

## Limitation

OWL animations are capped at 60 FPS, including on 90/120 Hz displays. Higher frame rates require an Omalauncher engine change.
