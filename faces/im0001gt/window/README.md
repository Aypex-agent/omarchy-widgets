# Window

A full-screen home page: the time, the weather where you are, and a painted sky for this hour. Colors follow the launcher theme. It asks the network for the weather and runs no commands.

## The temperature bars

On a wide screen, about 600dp across and up, each of the five days has a vertical bar between its high and its low. Every bar uses the same scale, from the coldest low to the warmest high across those five days. A longer bar is a wider swing between that day's low and high, and a mild day is a short mark on that shared scale. The number above the bar is the high. The number below it is the low. They use the same type. Today is drawn in the accent color. The percent under the low is the chance of precipitation.

On a narrow screen the same five days are listed without the bars.

## The sky

The painting follows the time of day and the current weather. The sun tracks today's sunrise and sunset, and the moon crosses the night. Rain, snow, fog, and thunder change what is on the glass. The line under the picture changes with the hour and the weather.
