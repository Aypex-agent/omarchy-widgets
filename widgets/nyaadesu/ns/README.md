# NS

Dutch railways (NS) for your home screen: whether the off-peak discount is on, and departures from your station.

- **Discount banner:** green while the discount is on, amber in the hour before it starts or ends ("Wait 23 min for discount"), red during peak hours. Peak hours are Mon–Fri 06:30–09:00 and 16:00–18:30, except public holidays. The discount goes by your check-in time.
- **Departures:** time, delay, train type, destination and platform (yellow, orange if the platform changed, × if cancelled). Trains that end early or skip stops say so. Under each destination are the major cities it passes through.
- **Favourites:** up to 3 stations, like 🏠 and Work. Trains that stop there are shown in the accent colour, and the fastest way there gets a filled highlight. A red warning sign appears when NS reports a disruption on the way. Tap it for NS's explanation.
- **Several stations:** save up to 6 and tap the station name to switch.

## Setup

1. Get a free API key: create an account at https://apiportal.ns.nl and subscribe to the **Ns-App** product.
2. Tap the tile, paste the key and search for your station.

The key is kept encrypted on your phone and is only sent to NS. Works without Linux.
