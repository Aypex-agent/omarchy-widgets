# Bills Tracker

An offline recurring-bills tracker for OmaLauncher.

Keep track of upcoming bills, recurring payments and payment history in one convenient widget. No account or internet connection required.

## Features

- Add, edit and delete recurring bills
- Weekly, monthly and yearly billing frequencies
- Upcoming payments displayed in due-date order
- Resizable upcoming-payment list
- Record payments and review payment history
- Monthly bill totals and average monthly expenses
- Choose from 85 currencies, with country flags and currency symbols
- Read-only lock to prevent accidental changes
- Locally saved bills, payment history and preferences

## Defaults

- Currency: GBP
- Widget size: 3x3
- Minimum size: 3x2

The upcoming-payment list adjusts to the widget's height.

## Privacy

All information stays on your device.

No account, internet connection, API, bank integration or additional Linux packages are required.

The read-only lock prevents accidental edits; it is not password-based security.

Currency selection does not perform exchange-rate conversion.

## Compatibility

Requires OmaLauncher with OWL 11 or newer.
