# Notes

A simple, lightweight notes widget for OmaLauncher.

Create, edit, copy and delete notes directly from your home screen. Everything works offline, with your notes saved locally on your device.

## Features

- Create and edit notes quickly
- Copy notes with a single tap
- Delete notes with confirmation
- Lock notes to prevent accidental editing or deletion
- Preview notes directly on your home screen
- Automatically display more notes when the widget is resized
- Works offline without an account
- No external dependencies

## How to Use

1. Tap the widget to open your notes.
2. Select **+ New** to create a note.
3. Write your note and tap **Done**.
4. Use the icons to copy, edit or delete notes.
5. Tap the lock icon to protect your notes from changes.

## Privacy

Notes does not require an internet connection.

Notes are stored locally on your device using OmaLauncher's persistent widget state. No notes are transmitted to external services.

## Requirements

- OmaLauncher with OWL 11 support
- No additional packages required
